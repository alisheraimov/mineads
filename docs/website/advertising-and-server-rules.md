# Advertising & Server Rules

Draft · Effective [effective date]

These rules apply to the ads, landing pages, and Minecraft servers submitted to VoxelAds. They form part of the [Terms of Service](terms.md).

## Rules for ads

- Keep ads and landing pages suitable for players of all ages. Ads for gambling, adult content, alcohol, tobacco and vaping products, or illegal drugs are prohibited. Ads may also be rejected for hate, harassment, or graphic violence.
- Describe the offer accurately. Clearly identify what is being promoted and disclose material conditions. Avoid false claims, impersonation, hidden charges, and misleading or forced redirects.
- Send players to the destination described in the ad. Phishing, malware, credential collection through impersonation, and attempts to hide a prohibited destination from review are prohibited.
- Use only material you have permission to use. Under Minecraft’s rules, using gameplay to promote products, services, events, campaigns, or brands unrelated to Minecraft requires prior written approval from Mojang or Microsoft. Obtain any required approval before submitting an ad. [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)

## Rules for servers

- Connect only servers you own or are authorized to manage. Keep the listed server address and category accurate and allow moderators to review the server.
- Display ads in approved placements. Keep ads identifiable and avoid interfering with gameplay, giving a competitive advantage, or requiring players to click or interact with an ad to play.
- Tell players about VoxelAds and what information the integration sends before enabling collection. Follow applicable privacy and child protection requirements. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md) and [Publisher Quick Start](publisher-guide.md).
- Report genuine ad activity. Bots, fake players, fabricated impressions, coordinated or incentivized clicks, and attempts to bypass review, reporting, or frequency limits are prohibited.

## Reviews and reports

Check your creative or server details for the latest review decision. If your submission is rejected, address the reason shown before resubmitting. To report an ad, server, or suspected invalid traffic, contact [support contact] with the server name, date and time, and a short description. Include the ad text or a screenshot if available. Keep account credentials, setup tokens, and player identifiers private.
