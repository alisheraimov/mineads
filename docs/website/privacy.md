## Cookies and browser storage

VoxelAds stores a sign-in token in your browser’s local storage and uses a cookie to remember your light or dark appearance setting. Signing out clears the stored sign-in token. You can also clear browser storage and cookies in your browser settings. Clearing these may sign you out or reset your appearance preference.
