## Details to confirm before publication

- **Operator and contacts.** Supply the legal operator name, address, monitored support and privacy contacts, and effective dates. Confirm account eligibility, governing law, and dispute resolution for the markets served.
- **Billing and payouts.** Confirm funding methods, cancellation and refund terms, payout methods and timing, publisher share and fees, tax responsibilities, and how balances are handled when an account closes. The guides reflect the current UI, where self-service funding and Stripe Connect are disabled.
- **Privacy.** Confirm deployed vendors, data locations and transfers, retention periods, applicable legal bases and rights, and each party’s responsibilities for player data. Review website and infrastructure logging, browser storage, plugin behavior, and handling of children’s data before enabling server collection. The UK ICO and US FTC guidance address different jurisdictions. [ICO privacy notice guidance](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/individual-rights/the-right-to-be-informed/what-privacy-information-should-we-provide/) · [FTC COPPA guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-coppa-frequently-asked-questions)
- **Minecraft permissions.** Confirm that VoxelAds’ own deployment and eligible campaigns fit the applicable Minecraft rules and any required written approvals. [Minecraft Usage Guidelines](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/usage-guidelines)
